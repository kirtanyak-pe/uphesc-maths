Already repeated

These ideas came up in more than one paper.

  • Generators of a cyclic group of order n

    There are φ(n). For n = 8 that is 4.

    UPHESC 2025 · UPHESC 2026 · GDC 2025

  • Centre of a non-abelian group of order p³

    The centre has order p. There are p² + p − 1 conjugacy classes.

    UPHESC 2026 · GDC 2017 · GDC 2025

  • Degree of a splitting field over ℚ

    x⁴ + x² + 1 splits in ℚ(ω), degree 2. x³ − 2 has degree 6.

    UPHESC 2025 · GDC 2017

  • Q36Is Z5[x]/(x^2+2) a field, and its orderA

    Answer: field of order 25

    x^2+2 has no root mod 5 (squares mod 5: 0,1,4; -2=3), so irreducible; Z5[x]/(x^2+2) is a field of 25 elements.

    Sure

  • Q47Phi: Z[x] to Z6, f maps to f(1) mod 6: determine kernelB

    Answer: 6, x-1

    ker = {f : f(1) = 0 mod 6} = (6, x-1) (not principal).

    Sure

  • Q52Count elements of F27 satisfying x^n=1 (exponent OCR-uncertain)D

    Answer: 13

    F27* cyclic of order 26 = 2*13; number of x with x^13=1 is gcd(13,26)=13.

    Sure

  • Q56Group of order p^3 with center of order p: number of conjugacy classesB

    Answer: p^2

    |G|=p^3, |Z|=p: non-central classes have size p, count (p^3-p)/p = p^2-1, total p^2+p-1 (D4: 5). Not among options (p+2, p^2, p+1, p^3); p^2 is nearest.

    Not sure

    correct value p^2+p-1 not an option; p^2 nearest.

  • Q64G acts on itself by conjugation, all classes of size 1: G is abelian/trivial/simple/cyclicA

    Answer: abelian

    All conjugacy classes of size 1 iff G abelian (not necessarily trivial, cyclic or simple).

    Sure

  • Q72Number of generators of cyclic group of order 105B

    phi(105)=phi(3)phi(5)phi(7)=2*4*6=48 (checked)

    Sure

  • Q36Degree of splitting field of x⁴+x²+1 over QA

    x^4+x^2+1 = (x^2+x+1)(x^2-x+1); all roots are primitive 3rd/6th roots of unity, in Q(w); degree 2.

    Sure
