  • Q35LP constraints x+2y<=6, 2x+y<=6; objective ax+by optimal on edge joining two vertices; find ratio a:bA

    Answer: 1:2

    Vertices (0,0),(3,0),(2,2),(0,3). Alternate optima need objective parallel to an edge: x+2y=6 edge gives 1:2, 2x+y=6 edge gives 2:1. Both edges have integral (assignment-polytope-like) endpoints, so the clause does not discriminate. Picked 1:2.

    Not sure

    two edges give ratio 1:2 or 2:1; the assignment-relaxation clause does not distinguish. Picked A.

  • Q59Gauss-Seidel on diagonally dominant PDE-discretised system: convergence rate vs JacobiB

    Answer: Faster

    For diagonally dominant systems GS typically converges faster than Jacobi (rho_GS = rho_J^2 for consistently ordered matrices).

    Fairly sure

    "faster" is the usual textbook claim, not universal for all diagonally dominant systems.

  • Q65Primal has alternate optima, dual region non-degenerate: implication for dualA

    Answer: dual unique solution

    Primal alt. optima iff dual degenerate at optimum, so hypothesis (non-degenerate dual) is contradictory; dual is feasible and bounded (C, D out). Between unique/multiple, picked unique.

    Not sure

    question is internally inconsistent (alternate optima in primal implies degenerate dual); guess.

  • Q69Modified Euler unstable on stiff equation; condition for stability improvement with RK4D

    RK4 has a bounded stability region (|h*lambda| < ~2.78) so stiff problems need small step size to stay stable
