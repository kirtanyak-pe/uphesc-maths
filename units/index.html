Units at a glance

How each syllabus unit moved between the two UPHESC papers, and how often each topic shows up across all four papers.

What changed from 2025 to 2026

Most units held steady. Analysis fell sharply, while linear algebra, methods and number theory grew.

Every topic, every paper

Darker means a bigger share of that paper. GDC is the sister UPPSC exam with a similar syllabus, so it shows what UP examiners like to ask. GDC 2017 had 100 maths questions, the others 70.

All 10 units

  • 1Abstract algebra6–8expectedSteady

    Six or seven questions every time, mostly short calculations on groups and fields.

  • 2Linear algebra8–10expectedRising

    The biggest unit. It grew to 10 questions in 2026, with Jordan forms and quadratic forms.

  • 3Real and functional analysis5–8expectedFell in 2026

    Fell from 9 questions to 3 in 2026. The sister exam still asks it heavily, so expect it to bounce back.

  • 4Complex analysis6–7expectedSteady

    Six or seven questions, mostly residues, contour integrals and Möbius maps.

  • 5Topology and differential geometry6–8expectedSteady

    About half topology, half curves and surfaces. Manifolds are new in the syllabus and appeared in 2026.

  • 6Ordinary and partial differential equations7expectedSteady

    Exactly 7 in both papers. The same ideas repeat: singular solutions and PDE types.

  • 7Calculus of variations, integral equations, transforms6–8expectedRising

    Grew from 5 to 8. Integral equations alone went from 2 questions to 6.

  • 8Mechanics and fluid dynamics7expectedSteady

    Exactly 7 in both papers. Mostly Lagrangian and Hamiltonian ideas, with two or three fluid questions.

  • 9Operations research and numerical analysis7expectedSteady

    Seven or eight questions, roughly half linear programming and half numerical methods.

  • 10Number theory, Vedic maths and history6–7expectedRising

    Number theory itself went from 3 questions to 7, three of them on Legendre symbols.

Built from four real papers: UPHESC 2025 and 2026, and the sister UPPSC GDC papers of 2017 and 2025. 310 maths questions in all.
