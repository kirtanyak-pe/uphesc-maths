  • Q41(y-px)^2=p^2(1+p^2): nature of singular solution relative to general solutionB

    Answer: envelope of general solution

    Equation is Clairaut: y = px +/- p*sqrt(1+p^2). Singular solution exists (from x = -f'(p)) and is the envelope of the family of lines.

    Sure

  • Q51U_tt - c^2 u_xx transforms to canonical form using which methodB

    Answer: characteristic variables

    Hyperbolic u_tt - c^2 u_xx=0: xi=x-ct, eta=x+ct gives u_xi_eta=0.

    Sure

  • Q57Eigenvalues of y''+lambda y=0, y(0)=y(pi)=0C

    Answer: n^2

    y=sin(nx), lambda=n^2, n=1,2,...

    Sure

  • Q66Y'=y^(1/3), y(0)=0: unique, none, infinitely many, or exactly two solutionsC

    y'=y^(1/3), y(0)=0: y=0 and y=±(2x/3)^(3/2) all work, and can be glued at any x0 (non-Lipschitz at 0) -> infinitely many solutions

    Sure

  • Q74Classify PDE x^2 u_xx + 2xy u_xy + ... (elliptic/hyperbolic/parabolic/mixed)C

    A=x^2, B=xy, C=y^2: B^2-AC = x^2y^2 - x^2y^2 = 0 -> parabolic

    Sure

  • Q79(D^2-4D+4)z=0: form of complementary functionC

    (D-2)^2 z=0 -> z=(A+Bx)e^(2x) (A,B arbitrary functions of y)

    Sure

  • Q91Y'' + (1/x)y' type equation for x>0: evaluate W(x)*x (constant, x, x^2, 1/x) (OCR-uncertain)A

    Homogeneous y'+y/x=0 -> y1=c/x, Wronskian (Abel) W=c/x so W*x=constant

    Sure
