  1. Question 1Easy
    Sociometry, used to study social relationships in a group, was developed by

    Why

    Moreno introduced sociometry and the sociogram.
  2. Question 2Easy
    The concept of the Zone of Proximal Development is associated with

    Why

    Vygotsky defined the ZPD as the gap between what a learner can do alone and with guidance.
  3. Question 3Easy
    Discovery learning is associated with

    Why

    Bruner promoted learning by discovery and the spiral curriculum.
  4. Question 4Easy
    Which of the following is NOT a domain in Bloom’s taxonomy of educational objectives?

    Why

    Bloom’s three domains are cognitive, affective and psychomotor.
  5. Question 5Easy
    The arithmetic mean of the scores \(4, 6, 8, 10, 12\) is

    Why

    The sum is \(40\) and there are \(5\) scores, so the mean is \(8\).
  6. Question 6Medium
    The population standard deviation of the scores \(2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9\) is

    Why

    The mean is \(5\) and the squared deviations sum to \(32\). Population variance is \(32/8 = 4\), so the standard deviation is \(2\). Option \(4\) is the variance; \(2.14\) divides by \(n-1\) instead of \(n\).
  7. Question 7Medium
    The median of \(3, 7, 9, 12, 15\) is

    Why

    Five ordered values: the middle one is the third, which is \(9\). The mean is \(9.2\).
  8. Question 8Medium
    A coefficient of correlation can NOT have the value

    Why

    The coefficient \(r\) lies between \(-1\) and \(1\) inclusive.
  9. Question 9Medium
    Which of the following is a probability sampling method?

    Why

    Probability sampling gives every unit a known chance of selection: simple random, systematic, stratified and cluster. Quota, purposive and snowball are non-probability.
  10. Question 10Medium
    A teacher randomly assigns pupils to two teaching methods and compares their post-test scores. The research design is

    Why

    Random assignment and manipulation of the teaching method (independent variable) define an experiment.
  11. Question 11Hard
    Under the UGC regulations on academic integrity, a thesis with 45 per cent similarity falls in

    Why

    Up to 10 per cent carries no penalty; above 10 up to 40 is Level 1; above 40 up to 60 is Level 2; above 60 is Level 3.
  12. Question 12Medium
    Arrange the steps of the research process: (a) formulating a hypothesis, (b) collecting data, (c) reviewing literature, (d) stating the problem.

    Why

    The problem comes first, then the literature review, then the hypothesis, and then the data collection.
  13. Question 13Hard
    Assertion (A): A research hypothesis must be testable. Reason (R): A testable hypothesis can be supported or rejected by evidence. Choose the correct option.

    Why

    Testability means the claim can be checked against evidence, which is exactly why a hypothesis must be testable.
  14. Question 14Easy
    The target gross enrolment ratio in higher education under NEP 2020 is

    Why

    NEP 2020 aims at a 50 per cent gross enrolment ratio in higher education by 2035.
