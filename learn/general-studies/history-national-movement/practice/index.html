  1. Question 1Easy
    The ancient text on statecraft, the Arthashastra, is attributed to

    Why

    Kautilya, the minister of Chandragupta Maurya, is traditionally the author. Megasthenes wrote Indica.
  2. Question 2Medium
    The Kalinga war of Ashoka took place about

    Why

    Ashoka’s conquest of Kalinga, after which he turned to dhamma, is dated to about 261 BCE.
  3. Question 3Medium
    Separate electorates for Muslims were introduced by the

    Why

    The Morley-Minto reforms of 1909 gave separate electorates.
  4. Question 4Easy
    Rule over India passed from the East India Company to the British Crown by the

    Why

    After the 1857 revolt, the 1858 Act ended Company rule and created the office of Secretary of State.
  5. Question 5Medium
    The Lucknow Pact between the Indian National Congress and the Muslim League was signed in

    Why

    The pact came at the Lucknow session of 1916. The Muslim League was founded in 1906.
  6. Question 6Hard
    Arrange in order of date: (a) Chauri Chaura, (b) Rowlatt Act, (c) Champaran satyagraha, (d) Simon Commission arrives.

    Why

    Champaran 1917, Rowlatt Act 1919, Chauri Chaura 1922, Simon Commission arrived in 1928.
  7. Question 7Medium
    Gandhi withdrew which movement after the Chauri Chaura incident of 1922?

    Why

    The violence at Chauri Chaura (Gorakhpur) made him suspend the Non-Cooperation Movement.
  8. Question 8Easy
    The last Viceroy of British India was

    Why

    Mountbatten became Viceroy in March 1947 and the first Governor-General of independent India.
  9. Question 9Medium
    The Quit India Movement was launched on

    Why

    The resolution was passed at Bombay on 8 August 1942. 9 August 1925 is Kakori and 12 March 1930 is the Dandi March.
  10. Question 10Medium
    The Chinese pilgrim Fa-Hien visited India during the reign of

    Why

    Fa-Hien came in the Gupta period under Chandragupta II. Hiuen Tsang came in Harsha’s reign.
  11. Question 11Easy
    Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat in

    Why

    1526 began Mughal rule. 1556 is the Second Battle of Panipat.
  12. Question 12Hard
    The Instrument of Accession for Jammu and Kashmir was signed on 26 October 1947 by

    Why

    Maharaja Hari Singh signed the Instrument of Accession on 26 October 1947; Mountbatten accepted it on 27 October. The Nizam ruled Hyderabad.
  13. Question 13Hard
    Who was the Secretary of the States Department that worked with Sardar Patel on the integration of princely states?

    Why

    V. P. Menon was Patel’s secretary in the States Department and drafted the accession terms.
