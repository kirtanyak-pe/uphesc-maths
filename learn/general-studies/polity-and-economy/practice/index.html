  1. Question 1Easy
    A writ of habeas corpus is issued to

    Why

    Habeas corpus means ‘to have the body’. The other options describe mandamus, prohibition and quo warranto.
  2. Question 2Medium
    The voting age was lowered from 21 to 18 years by the

    Why

    The 61st Amendment Act, 1988 reduced the age.
  3. Question 3Easy
    Panchayati Raj institutions were given constitutional status by the

    Why

    The 73rd Amendment added Part IX; the 74th covers municipalities.
  4. Question 4Medium
    The Finance Commission is provided for in Article

    Why

    Article 280 creates it. Article 275 is grants-in-aid, 324 the Election Commission and 148 the CAG.
  5. Question 5Medium
    NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in

    Why

    NITI Aayog was formed on 1 January 2015.
  6. Question 6Medium
    In a Budget, revenue expenditure is 90, capital expenditure is 30, revenue receipts are 70 and non-debt capital receipts are 10 (all in thousand crore rupees). The fiscal deficit is

    Why

    Fiscal deficit = total expenditure (120) minus receipts excluding borrowing (70+10) = 40. The value 50 forgets the non-debt capital receipts and 20 is the revenue deficit.
  7. Question 7Hard
    With the same figures and interest payments of 15, the primary deficit is

    Why

    Primary deficit = fiscal deficit minus interest payments = 40 - 15 = 25.
  8. Question 8Medium
    Unemployment arising from a mismatch between workers’ skills and the skills that jobs require is

    Why

    Structural unemployment comes from a skills or location gap, not from a downturn or the season.
  9. Question 9Medium
    The basic structure doctrine was laid down in

    Why

    Kesavananda Bharati held that Parliament cannot amend the basic structure. Golaknath had said that fundamental rights cannot be amended.
  10. Question 10Easy
    The Directive Principles of State Policy were borrowed from the Constitution of

    Why

    The idea came from Ireland. The US gave fundamental rights, Canada the strong federation and Australia the concurrent list.
  11. Question 11Medium
    The Reserve Bank of India began functioning in

    Why

    RBI was set up on 1 April 1935 and nationalised in 1949; 1969 is the first bank nationalisation.
  12. Question 12Medium
    When the Reserve Bank raises the cash reserve ratio, the lending capacity of banks

    Why

    Banks must keep a larger share of deposits with the RBI, so they have less to lend, which reduces money supply.
  13. Question 13Medium
    The GST Council is chaired by the

    Why

    Article 279A provides that the Union Finance Minister chairs the Council.
  14. Question 14Medium
    Match the amendment with its subject. (P) 42nd (Q) 44th (R) 52nd (S) 86th. Subject: 1 Right to education, 2 Anti-defection, 3 Fundamental duties, 4 Right to property removed.

    Why

    42nd (1976) added the duties; 44th (1978) removed property from fundamental rights; 52nd (1985) added the anti-defection law; 86th (2002) added Article 21A.
  15. Question 15Hard
    Consider: (1) The Finance Commission is a constitutional body. (2) The Planning Commission had constitutional status. (3) NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission. Which statements are correct?

    Why

    The Planning Commission was set up by a Cabinet resolution in 1950 and had no constitutional status, so (2) is false.
