This is a fact sheet of the most-tested static facts, one line per area.

ICT. \(1\text{ byte} = 8\text{ bits}\); \(1\text{ KB}=1024\) bytes; IPv4 32 bits, IPv6 128 bits; \(25_{10}=11001_2\); \(2F_{16}=47_{10}\). TCP reliable transport, HTTP hypertext, FTP files, DNS names to addresses, SMTP sends mail. Bandwidth is the maximum data rate. Digital divide is unequal access.

Teaching and research. Moreno: sociometry. Vygotsky: zone of proximal development. Bruner: discovery learning. Bloom: cognitive, affective, psychomotor. Experimental research controls variables to find cause. Probability sampling: simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster. Correlation \(-1\le r\le 1\). UGC plagiarism levels: up to 10 per cent none, 10-40 Level 1, 40-60 Level 2, above 60 Level 3.

Environment. Energy passes up trophic levels at about 10 per cent. Ramsar wetlands, Montreal ozone, Basel hazardous waste, CITES endangered-species trade, Cartagena living modified organisms. AQI: 0-50 Good, 51-100 Satisfactory, 101-200 Moderate, 201-300 Poor, 301-400 Very Poor, 401-500 Severe. Kunming-Montreal framework: 30 per cent by 2030.

Science and space. Kepler: \(T^2\propto a^3\). Terrestrial planets form by accretion of planetesimals. Heavy elements come from supernovae. Light from the Sun takes about 8.3 minutes. CMB is about 2.7 K. Chandrayaan-3 landed on 23 August 2023.

History. Megasthenes at Chandragupta Maurya’s court. Dyarchy 1919. Provincial autonomy 1935. INC 1885, League 1906, Lucknow Pact 1916. Quit India 8 August 1942. Mountbatten Plan 3 June 1947; AICC accepted Partition 14-15 June. States joined by Instrument of Accession.

Polity. Constitution in force 26 January 1950. Article 21A education (86th Amendment). Article 32 remedies, Article 13 judicial review. Duties in the 42nd Amendment (1976). 73rd and 74th local bodies, 97th cooperatives, 101st GST. Education is on the Concurrent List; public health on the State List.

Economy. Fiscal deficit = total expenditure minus receipts excluding borrowing. Finance Commission (Article 280) shares taxes; Planning Commission (1950) was replaced by NITI Aayog (2015). Disguised unemployment: extra workers add nothing. Tight monetary policy lowers inflation.

Uttar Pradesh. 18 divisions, 75 districts; capital Lucknow; one national park (Dudhwa); four World Heritage sites (Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, and Sarnath since July 2026).
