  1. Question 1Easy
    By the ten per cent law, if producers store \(10000\) J of energy, the energy available at the secondary consumer level is about

    Why

    Producers to primary consumers: \(10000\times 0.1 = 1000\) J. Primary to secondary: \(1000\times 0.1 = 100\) J.
  2. Question 2Easy
    The most abundant gas in the Earth’s atmosphere by volume is

    Why

    Dry air is about 78 per cent nitrogen and 21 per cent oxygen.
  3. Question 3Medium
    On India’s National Air Quality Index, the band \(201\) to \(300\) is called

    Why

    The bands are Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderate 101-200, Poor 201-300, Very Poor 301-400, Severe 401-500.
  4. Question 4Easy
    The Montreal Protocol (1987) regulates

    Why

    It phases out CFCs and similar substances. CITES covers species trade, Basel covers hazardous waste and Ramsar covers wetlands.
  5. Question 5Medium
    Match the treaty with its subject. (P) Ramsar (Q) Basel (R) CITES (S) Cartagena. Subject: 1 Living modified organisms, 2 Trade in endangered species, 3 Hazardous waste movement, 4 Wetlands.

    Why

    Ramsar protects wetlands, Basel controls hazardous waste, CITES regulates trade in endangered species and the Cartagena Protocol covers living modified organisms.
  6. Question 6Easy
    Biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) is used as a measure of

    Why

    A high BOD means a lot of organic matter that microbes will break down using dissolved oxygen.
  7. Question 7Easy
    The ozone layer is found mainly in the

    Why

    The ozone layer lies in the stratosphere, about 15 to 35 km above the surface.
  8. Question 8Medium
    Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas?

    Why

    Argon is an inert gas that does not absorb infrared radiation in the relevant way.
  9. Question 9Medium
    The main temperature goal of the Paris Agreement (2015) is to hold global warming well below

    Why

    The Agreement aims for well below \(2^\circ\)C and efforts to limit warming to \(1.5^\circ\)C.
  10. Question 10Easy
    The idea that development should meet present needs without compromising the needs of future generations is called

    Why

    This is the Brundtland definition, from Our Common Future (1987).
  11. Question 11Medium
    Biomagnification refers to

    Why

    Substances like DDT build up in tissues and become more concentrated up the food chain.
  12. Question 12Hard
    Assertion (A): Excess nutrient runoff into a lake can reduce its dissolved oxygen. Reason (R): Nutrient enrichment causes algal blooms whose decomposition consumes oxygen. Choose the correct option.

    Why

    This is eutrophication: nutrients feed algal blooms, and the dead algae are decomposed by oxygen-consuming microbes.
  13. Question 13Medium
    Carbon trading is a system in which

    Why

    Carbon markets trade allowances or credits for greenhouse gas emissions.
  14. Question 14Hard
    Consider: (1) The ten per cent law describes energy transfer between trophic levels. (2) Nitrogen is the second most abundant gas in air. (3) Ramsar concerns wetlands. Which statements are correct?

    Why

    Nitrogen is the most abundant gas, not the second, so (2) is false.
