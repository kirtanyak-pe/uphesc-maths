  1. Question 1Easy
    The 18th G20 Leaders’ Summit (2023) was hosted by

    Why

    India held the G20 presidency in 2023 and the summit was in New Delhi. Bali was the 17th (2022); Rio de Janeiro the 19th; Johannesburg the 20th.
  2. Question 2Easy
    India’s Chandrayaan-3 lander touched down on the Moon in

    Why

    Chandrayaan-3 was launched on 14 July 2023 and landed on 23 August 2023. September 2019 is the Chandrayaan-2 lander failure and November 2008 the Chandrayaan-1 impact probe.
  3. Question 3Easy
    Aditya-L1, launched by ISRO on 2 September 2023, is a mission to study the

    Why

    Aditya-L1 is India’s first solar observatory, positioned near the Sun-Earth Lagrange point L1. Mars is Mangalyaan; the Moon is Chandrayaan.
  4. Question 4Medium
    Match the organisation with its core focus. (P) WHO (Q) WTO (R) IAEA (S) UNEP. Focus: 1 Environmental policy, 2 Nuclear regulation, 3 Public health, 4 Global trade governance.

    Why

    WHO deals with public health, WTO with trade rules, IAEA with nuclear safety and safeguards, UNEP with environmental policy.
  5. Question 5Medium
    The 2023 Nobel Peace Prize was awarded to

    Why

    Narges Mohammadi, the Iranian human rights activist, received the 2023 prize. Maria Ressa shared the 2021 prize; Ales Bialiatski shared the 2022 prize.
  6. Question 6Medium
    D. Gukesh became the youngest World Chess Champion in

    Why

    Gukesh defeated Ding Liren in the match played in Singapore and won the title in December 2024.
  7. Question 7Medium
    Arrange the G20 summit hosts in the order of their summits (earliest first): (a) New Delhi, (b) Johannesburg, (c) Bali, (d) Rio de Janeiro.

    Why

    17th Bali (2022), 18th New Delhi (2023), 19th Rio de Janeiro (2024), 20th Johannesburg (2025).
  8. Question 8Easy
    The Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on

    Why

    It was launched on Gandhi Jayanti, 2 October 2014.
  9. Question 9Easy
    Which body regulates the securities market in India?

    Why

    SEBI regulates securities markets; RBI regulates banks and money; IRDAI insurance; PFRDA pensions.
  10. Question 10Medium
    In the Union Budget 2025-26, registration of gig workers for identity cards and health cover was announced on which portal?

    Why

    The Budget announced identity cards and e-Shram registration for online platform workers, with health cover under PM-JAY. UMANG, DigiLocker and GeM are other government platforms.
  11. Question 11Medium
    The 19th Asian Games, postponed from 2022, were held in 2023 in

    Why

    Hangzhou, China hosted the Games from September to October 2023. Jakarta hosted in 2018, Incheon in 2014 and Guangzhou in 2010.
  12. Question 12Hard
    At the New Delhi G20 summit (2023), which bloc was admitted as a permanent member of the G20?

    Why

    The African Union joined as a permanent member at the New Delhi summit. The European Union was already a member; ASEAN and BRICS are not G20 members.
  13. Question 13Easy
    ISRO’s planned programme to send Indian astronauts into low Earth orbit is called

    Why

    Gaganyaan is the human spaceflight programme. Mangalyaan was the Mars orbiter, Aditya the solar mission and Shukrayaan a proposed Venus mission.
