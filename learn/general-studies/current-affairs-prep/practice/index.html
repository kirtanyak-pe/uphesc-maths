  1. Question 1Medium
    D. Gukesh became the youngest World Chess Champion in

    Why

    Gukesh defeated Ding Liren in the match played in Singapore and won the title in December 2024.
  2. Question 2Hard
    Arrange the G20 summit hosts in the order of their summits (earliest first): (a) New Delhi, (b) Johannesburg, (c) Bali, (d) Rio de Janeiro.

    Why

    17th Bali (2022), 18th New Delhi (2023), 19th Rio de Janeiro (2024), 20th Johannesburg (2025).
  3. Question 3Easy
    Aditya-L1 is stationed near a point about 1.5 million km from Earth towards the Sun. That point is called

    Why

    Aditya-L1 sits near the Sun-Earth Lagrange point L1, which gives an unobstructed view of the Sun. L2 lies on the far side of Earth and is used by telescopes looking outwards.
  4. Question 4Medium
    Match the organisation with its core focus. (P) FAO (Q) ILO (R) UNESCO (S) IMF. Focus: 1 Education, science and culture, 2 Labour standards, 3 Food and agriculture, 4 Monetary stability.

    Why

    FAO deals with food and agriculture, the ILO with labour standards, UNESCO with education, science and culture, and the IMF with monetary stability.
  5. Question 5Easy
    Who was sworn in as the 53rd Chief Justice of India on 24 November 2025?

    Why

    Justice Surya Kant took the oath on 24 November 2025. He succeeded Justice Gavai; Justices Chandrachud and Khanna were earlier Chief Justices.
  6. Question 6Easy
    India won its first ICC Women’s World Cup title in November 2025 by beating

    Why

    In the final at Navi Mumbai on 2 November 2025 India beat South Africa by 52 runs.
  7. Question 7Medium
    India won the men’s T20 World Cup final on 8 March 2026 at Ahmedabad by defeating

    Why

    India beat New Zealand by 96 runs, scoring 255 for 5, for a third title and the first back-to-back wins in the tournament.
  8. Question 8Medium
    The 2025 Nobel Peace Prize was awarded to

    Why

    Machado of Venezuela won the 2025 prize. Mohammadi won in 2023; the 2026 prize went to Navi Pillay.
  9. Question 9Medium
    The number of awards in the Padma Awards list announced in January 2026 is

    Why

    The 2026 list has 131 awards: 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri.
  10. Question 10Medium
    The document adopted at the India AI Impact Summit in New Delhi in February 2026 is called the

    Why

    The non-binding New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact was adopted at the summit. Bletchley (2023), Seoul (2024) and Paris (2025) were earlier AI summits.
  11. Question 11Easy
    NISAR, launched on GSLV-F16 on 30 July 2025, is a joint Earth-observation satellite of ISRO and

    Why

    NISAR is the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar satellite. NASA provided the L-band radar and ISRO the S-band radar.
  12. Question 12Hard
    In the 2027 Census, the reference date for most of India is

    Why

    Population enumeration is planned for February 2027 with a reference date of 1 March 2027. The earlier reference date of 1 October 2026 applies only to Ladakh and snow-bound areas.
  13. Question 13Hard
    At COP31 in Antalya (9-20 November 2026), the formal negotiations are presided over by

    Why

    Turkiye hosts the conference and holds the COP presidency, but Australia (Chris Bowen) is President of Negotiations, an unusual split agreed at COP30.
  14. Question 14Medium
    In the Bihar assembly election of November 2025 (243 seats), the NDA won

    Why

    The NDA won 202 of 243 seats. A majority needs 122, which is the distractor.
  15. Question 15Medium
    India finished at which position in the medal table of the 2026 Asian Games at Aichi-Nagoya, with 85 medals?

    Why

    India won 21 gold, 27 silver and 37 bronze for 85 medals and finished fourth, ahead of Uzbekistan on silver medals.
