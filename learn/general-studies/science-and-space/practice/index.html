  1. Question 1Medium
    A planet moves around the Sun at a mean distance of \(4\) AU. By Kepler’s third law its period is

    Why

    \(T^2 = a^3\) gives \(T = 4^{3/2} = 8\) years. Option \(16\) uses \(T=a^2\); option \(2\) uses \(T=\sqrt{a}\).
  2. Question 2Hard
    Jupiter’s mean distance from the Sun is about \(5.2\) AU. Its orbital period in years is nearest to

    Why

    \(T = 5.2^{1.5} \approx 11.9\) years. The square \(27.0\) and the root \(2.3\) come from using the wrong power.
  3. Question 3Medium
    Light takes about how long to travel from the Sun to the Earth (distance about \(1.5\times 10^{8}\) km)?

    Why

    \(1.5\times10^{8}/3\times10^{5} = 500\) s, which is about \(8.3\) min. The \(1.3\) s value is the Moon’s light time.
  4. Question 4Medium
    A solid block floats in water with three quarters of its volume submerged. The density of the block is (water density \(1000\) kg/m\(^3\))

    Why

    For floating, density of block \(=\) fraction submerged \(\times\) density of water \(= 0.75\times 1000 = 750\).
  5. Question 5Easy
    Which of the following is a terrestrial planet?

    Why

    The terrestrial planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars.
  6. Question 6Easy
    Which planet has the shortest orbital period?

    Why

    Period increases with distance, and Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
  7. Question 7Medium
    The cosmic microwave background has a present temperature of about

    Why

    The CMB is relic radiation from the hot early universe and is cooled to about \(2.7\) K.
  8. Question 8Medium
    The Chandrayaan-3 lander touched down near the Moon’s

    Why

    It landed at a high southern latitude on 23 August 2023.
  9. Question 9Medium
    India’s first satellite, Aryabhata, was launched in

    Why

    Aryabhata was launched on 19 April 1975 using a Soviet launch vehicle.
  10. Question 10Easy
    The energy of the Sun comes mainly from

    Why

    Hydrogen nuclei fuse in the core to form helium, releasing energy.
  11. Question 11Easy
    The speed of light in vacuum is about

    Why

    \(3\times10^{8}\) m/s. Sound in air travels at about \(340\) m/s.
  12. Question 12Medium
    The SI unit of power is

    Why

    Power is energy per unit time: \(1\text{ W} = 1\text{ J/s}\).
  13. Question 13Easy
    The three laws of planetary motion were formulated by

    Why

    Kepler derived the laws from Tycho Brahe’s observations. Copernicus proposed the Sun-centred model.
  14. Question 14Hard
    AlphaFold, an AI system from DeepMind, is best known for predicting

    Why

    AlphaFold predicts protein structure from the amino acid sequence.
