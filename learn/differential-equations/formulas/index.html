First-order ODEs

  • Exact: \(M\,dx+N\,dy=0\) with \(M_y=N_x\); solution \(F=c\), \(F_x=M\), \(F_y=N\).
  • Integrating factor: \(\mu=e^{\int\frac{M_y-N_x}{N}dx}\) if that ratio depends on \(x\) only; \(\mu=e^{\int\frac{N_x-M_y}{M}dy}\) if \(\frac{N_x-M_y}{M}\) depends on \(y\) only; \(\mu=\frac1{Mx+Ny}\) for homogeneous \(M,N\).
  • Linear: \(y'+Py=Q\), \(y=e^{-\int P}\big(\int Qe^{\int P}+c\big)\). Bernoulli: \(y'+Py=Qy^n\), \(v=y^{1-n}\), \(v'+(1-n)Pv=(1-n)Q\).
  • Clairaut \(y=px+f(p)\): general \(y=cx+f(c)\); singular \(x=-f'(p)\), \(y=px+f(p)\). Lagrange \(y=x\varphi(p)+\psi(p)\): linear in \(x\) over \(p\); lines with \(p=\varphi(p)\) are extra solutions.
  • Singular solution: envelope; from the \(p\)-discriminant or \(c\)-discriminant, then verify it satisfies the ODE and is not in the family. Envelope appears once in each discriminant; node twice and cusp three times in the \(c\)-discriminant; tac-locus twice in the \(p\)-discriminant.
  • Orthogonal trajectories: \(y'\to-1/y'\); polar: \(dr/d\theta\to-r^2d\theta/dr\).
  • Peano: \(f\) continuous implies a solution exists on \(\lvert x-x_0\rvert\le\min(a,b/M)\). Picard-Lindelof: \(f\) continuous and Lipschitz in \(y\) implies a unique solution. \(f_y\) bounded implies Lipschitz; Lipschitz does not need \(f_y\) to exist.
  • Picard iterates: \(y_{n+1}=y_0+\int_{x_0}^xf(t,y_n(t))\,dt\).
  • Standard IVPs: \(y'=y^{1/3}\), \(y(0)=0\) has infinitely many solutions. \(xy'=4y\): \(y(0)=2\) none, \(y(0)=0\) infinitely many.

Linear ODEs

  • Solution space of \(L[y]=0\) has dimension \(n\); \(y=y_h+y_p\).
  • \(W=\det\big(y_i^{(j)}\big)\); Abel: \(W=W(x_0)\exp\left(-\int p_1\right)\); for \(y''+py'+qy=0\), \(W=Ce^{-\int p}\); no \(y'\) term means \(W\) constant. \(W\equiv0\) implies dependence only for solutions of one linear equation.
  • Constant coefficients: auxiliary \(f(m)=0\); repeated root \(k\): \((c_0+\dots+c_{k-1}x^{k-1})e^{mx}\); complex \(\alpha\pm i\beta\): \(e^{\alpha x}(A\cos\beta x+B\sin\beta x)\).
  • PI: \(\frac{e^{ax}}{f(a)}\); resonance \(\frac{x^ke^{ax}}{f^{(k)}(a)}\); \(\frac{1}{D^2+a^2}\sin ax=-\frac{x\cos ax}{2a}\), \(\frac1{D^2+a^2}\cos ax=\frac{x\sin ax}{2a}\); \(\frac{1}{f(D)}e^{ax}V=e^{ax}\frac1{f(D+a)}V\).
  • Variation of parameters: \(y_p=-y_1\int\frac{y_2r}{W}+y_2\int\frac{y_1r}{W}\).
  • Cauchy-Euler: \(x=e^t\), \(m(m-1)+am+b=0\); repeated root: \(x^m\ln x\); complex: \(x^\alpha\cos(\beta\ln x)\), \(x^\alpha\sin(\beta\ln x)\).
  • Reduction of order: \(y_2=y_1\int\frac{e^{-\int p}}{y_1^2}dx\).
  • Systems \(x'=Ax\): \(e^{\lambda t}v\); defective: \(e^{\lambda t}(tv+w)\) with \((A-\lambda)w=v\); \(\det\Phi=\det\Phi(0)e^{t\operatorname{tr}A}\).
  • Power series: ordinary point, radius at least the distance to the nearest complex singularity. Frobenius: indicial \(r(r-1)+p_0r+q_0=0\); equal roots give a \(\ln\) term.

Sturm-Liouville

  • \((py')'+qy+\lambda wy=0\); conversion \(p=e^{\int a_1/a_0}\), \(w=p\,a_3/a_0\).
  • Eigenvalues real and simple; eigenfunctions orthogonal with weight \(w\); \(\phi_n\) has \(n-1\) interior zeros.
  • \(y''+\lambda y=0\) on \([0,L]\): Dirichlet \((n\pi/L)^2\) (\(\sin\), \(n\ge1\)); Neumann \((n\pi/L)^2\) (\(\cos\), \(n\ge0\)); mixed \(\left(\frac{(2n-1)\pi}{2L}\right)^2\); periodic: two eigenfunctions per eigenvalue.
  • Sturm separation: independent solutions have interlacing zeros. Comparison: \(q_2>q_1\) implies \(z\) has a zero between consecutive zeros of \(y\).
  • Green’s function of \((py')'+qy=f\): \(G=\frac{u_1(x_<)u_2(x_>)}{pW}\); continuous, symmetric, \(G_x\) jumps by \(1/p\). Fredholm alternative: non-trivial homogeneous solution \(\phi\) requires \(\int f\phi=0\).

First-order PDEs

  • Lagrange \(Pp+Qq=R\) (\(p=z_x\), \(q=z_y\)): \(\frac{dx}{P}=\frac{dy}{Q}=\frac{dz}{R}\); integrals \(u=c_1\), \(v=c_2\); general solution \(F(u,v)=0\). Multipliers: \(\frac{l\,dx+m\,dy+n\,dz}{lP+mQ+nR}\).
  • Complete integral has two arbitrary constants; general integral from \(b=\varphi(a)\) (envelope); singular integral from \(f=f_a=f_b=0\).
  • Standard forms: \(f(p,q)=0\): \(z=ax+by+c\) with \(f(a,b)=0\); Clairaut \(z=px+qy+f(p,q)\): \(z=ax+by+f(a,b)\); \(f(z,p,q)=0\): \(z=\phi(x+ay)\); \(f_1(x,p)=f_2(y,q)=a\).
  • Charpit for \(F(x,y,z,p,q)=0\): \(\frac{dp}{F_x+pF_z}=\frac{dq}{F_y+qF_z}=\frac{dz}{-pF_p-qF_q}=\frac{dx}{-F_p}=\frac{dy}{-F_q}\).

Higher-order linear PDEs and classification

  • \(D=\partial_x\), \(D'=\partial_y\). \((D-mD')z=0\): \(z=\phi(y+mx)\); repeated: \(\phi_1(y+mx)+x\phi_2(y+mx)\); \((D-mD'-c)z=0\): \(z=e^{cx}\phi(y+mx)\).
  • PI: \(\frac{e^{ax+by}}{F(a,b)}\) if \(F(a,b)\ne0\); \(\frac{\sin(ax+by)}{F(-a^2,-ab,-b^2)}\) if non-zero; polynomial right sides by series expansion; shift \(\frac1Fe^{ax+by}V=e^{ax+by}\frac{1}{F(D+a,D'+b)}V\).
  • \(Au_{xx}+2Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}+\dots\): hyperbolic if \(B^2-AC>0\), parabolic if \(=0\), elliptic if \(<0\) (with \(Au_{xx}+Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}\) use \(B^2-4AC\)). Characteristics: \(A\,dy^2-2B\,dx\,dy+C\,dx^2=0\).
  • Canonical forms: \(u_{\xi\eta}=\Phi\) (hyperbolic), \(u_{\eta\eta}=\Phi\) (parabolic), \(u_{\xi\xi}+u_{\eta\eta}=\Phi\) (elliptic). Wave equation: \(\xi=x-ct\), \(\eta=x+ct\), \(u=f(x-ct)+g(x+ct)\).
  • \(u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0\): \(u=f(x+iy)+g(x-iy)\).

Separation of variables

  • Heat \(u_t=ku_{xx}\), \(u(0,t)=u(L,t)=0\): \(u=\sum b_ne^{-kn^2\pi^2t/L^2}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}\), \(b_n=\frac2L\int_0^Lf\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}dx\). Insulated ends: cosine series, limit \(a_0/2\).
  • Wave \(u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}\), fixed ends: \(u=\sum\left(a_n\cos\frac{n\pi ct}{L}+b_n\sin\frac{n\pi ct}{L}\right)\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}\), \(a_n=\frac2L\int f\sin\), \(b_n=\frac{2}{n\pi c}\int g\sin\). d’Alembert: \(u=\frac12[f(x+ct)+f(x-ct)]+\frac1{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct}g\).
  • Laplace on a rectangle: \(\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a}\sinh\frac{n\pi y}{a}\) terms; on a disc: \(u=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum\left(\frac ra\right)^n(a_n\cos n\theta+b_n\sin n\theta)\), centre value \(a_0/2\).
  • Separation constant: \(X''/X=-\lambda\) gives the Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue problem; Dirichlet gives sine, Neumann gives cosine.
