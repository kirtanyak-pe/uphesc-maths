What this unit covers

Unit 6 splits into an ODE half and a PDE half.

ODEs (topics 1-3).

  • First-order ODEs: exact equations, integrating factors, linear and Bernoulli equations, Clairaut and Lagrange equations, singular solutions, orthogonal trajectories, and the existence and uniqueness theorems (Peano, Picard-Lindelof, the Lipschitz condition, Picard iterates).
  • Linear ODEs: the Wronskian and Abel’s formula, constant-coefficient equations with the operator shortcuts for the particular integral, variation of parameters, Cauchy-Euler equations, reduction of order, linear systems, and power series at the statement level (Frobenius).
  • Sturm-Liouville problems: eigenvalues and eigenfunctions for \(y''+\lambda y=0\) with different boundary conditions, orthogonality with weight, Sturm comparison, and Green’s function.

PDEs (topics 4-6).

  • First-order PDEs: Lagrange’s method and Charpit’s method with the standard forms.
  • Higher-order linear PDEs: constant-coefficient equations (complementary function and particular integral), and classification and canonical forms of second-order equations.
  • Separation of variables: the Laplace, heat and wave equations with their series solutions.

High-yield list

  • IVP counting. No / unique / infinitely many solutions: know \(y'=y^{1/3}\) (infinitely many) and \(xy'=ky\), \(y(0)=y_0\) (none if \(y_0\ne0\), infinite if \(y_0=0\), \(k>0\)).
  • Peano versus Picard-Lindelof. Continuity gives existence; Lipschitz in \(y\) adds uniqueness.
  • Clairaut singular solution. \(x=-f'(p)\); singular solution is the envelope of the general solution.
  • \(p\)- and \(c\)-discriminants, and the check that the candidate satisfies the ODE and is not in the family.
  • Abel’s formula and the fact that \(y''+qy=0\) has constant Wronskian.
  • Operator shortcuts for the particular integral, including the shift rule and resonance cases (\(\frac{x^2e^{ax}}{2}\), \(-\frac{x\cos ax}{2a}\)).
  • Cauchy-Euler with repeated or complex roots.
  • Eigenvalues of \(y''+\lambda y=0\) for Dirichlet, Neumann and mixed boundary conditions; properties of Sturm-Liouville problems.
  • Classification by \(B^2-AC\) and the complementary function \((D-mD')\) form for PDEs.
  • Lagrange and Charpit: auxiliary equations, complete integral of Clairaut-type PDEs.
  • Separation of variables: form of series solutions for heat, wave and Laplace equations, and which boundary conditions give sine or cosine series.

Official syllabus

Existence and uniqueness of solutions of initial value problems for first order ordinary differential equations, singular solutions of first order ordinary differential equations, General theory of homogeneous and non-homogenous linear ordinary differential equations, Strum-Liouville boundary value problem. Lagrange and Charpit methods for solving first order partial differential equations, General solution of higher order partial differential equations with constant coefficients, classification of second order partial differential equations, Method of separation of variables for Laplace, Heat and Wave equations.
