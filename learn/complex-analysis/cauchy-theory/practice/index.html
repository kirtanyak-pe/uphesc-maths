  1. Question 1Medium
    The value of \(\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=2}\frac{e^{2z}}{(z+1)^4}\,dz\) is

    Why

    Cauchy’s formula for derivatives with \(f=e^{2z}\), \(a=-1\), \(n=3\): \(\frac{2\pi i}{3!}f'''(-1)=\frac{2\pi i}{6}\cdot8e^{-2}=\frac{8\pi i}{3e^2}\).
  2. Question 2Easy
    \(\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{dz}{z}=\)

    Why

    Parametrise \(z=e^{i\theta}\): \(\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{ie^{i\theta}}{e^{i\theta}}d\theta=2\pi i\). This is not \(0\) because \(1/z\) is not analytic at \(0\).
  3. Question 3Medium
    \(\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=2}\bar z\,dz=\)

    Why

    On \(|z|=2\), \(\bar z=4/z\), so the integral is \(4\cdot2\pi i=8\pi i\). (Also \(2i\times\) area \(=2i\cdot4\pi\).) \(\bar z\) is not analytic, so Cauchy’s theorem does not apply.
  4. Question 4Medium
    Let \(f\) be entire with \(|f(z)|\le3|z|^{2}\) for all \(|z|\ge1\). Then \(f\) is

    Why

    Cauchy estimates: \(|f^{(n)}(0)|\le n!M(R)/R^n\le n!\,3R^{2-n}\to0\) as \(R\to\infty\) for \(n\ge3\). So the Taylor series stops at degree \(2\); \(f\) is a polynomial of degree at most \(2\) (e.g. \(f=0\) or \(f=z\) fit).
  5. Question 5Medium
    Which statement is true for an entire function \(f\) satisfying \(|f(z)|\le|z|^3\) for all \(z\in\mathbb C\)?

    Why

    By Liouville-type estimates \(f\) is a polynomial of degree at most \(3\). \(f(0)=0\) and \(|f(z)|\le|z|^3\) near \(0\) forces \(f(0)=f'(0)=f''(0)=0\). So \(f=cz^3\), and \(|c|\le1\) from the inequality. This is the 2026 Q86 pattern.

  8. Question 8Hard
    The maximum of \(|z^2-1|\) over the closed disc \(|z|\le1\) is

    Why

    The maximum occurs on the boundary. With \(z=e^{i\theta}\), \(|z^2-1|=|e^{2i\theta}-1|=2|\sin\theta|\), whose maximum is \(2\) at \(z=\pm i\).
  9. Question 9Medium
    Let \(f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D\) be analytic on the open unit disc with \(f(0)=0\) and \(f'(0)=1\) (in modulus: \(|f'(0)|=1\)). Then

    Why

    Schwarz lemma equality case: \(|f'(0)|=1\) forces \(f(z)=e^{i\alpha}z\), a rotation. (Strict inequality \(|f(z)|<|z|\) holds only when \(f\) is not a rotation.)
  10. Question 10Easy
    The mean value property for analytic \(f\) on a neighbourhood of \(|z-a|\le r\) states \(f(a)=\)

    Why

    Put \(z=a+re^{i\theta}\) in Cauchy’s formula: \(f(a)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint\frac{f(z)}{z-a}dz=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}f(a+re^{i\theta})d\theta\).
  11. Question 11Medium
    Let \(\gamma\) be the circle \(|z|=2\) traversed once counter-clockwise. The ML-inequality gives the best bound \(\left|\oint_\gamma\frac{dz}{z^2+1}\right|\le\)

    Why

    On \(|z|=2\): \(|z^2+1|\ge|z|^2-1=3\), so \(M=\frac13\), and \(L=4\pi\). Bound: \(\frac{4\pi}3\). (The exact value is \(0\), since the residues \(\pm\frac1{2i}\) cancel.)
  12. Question 12Medium
    \(\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{\cos z}{z(z-2)}\,dz=\)

    Why

    Only \(z=0\) lies inside. Take \(g(z)=\frac{\cos z}{z-2}\): integral \(=2\pi i\,g(0)=2\pi i\cdot\frac1{-2}=-\pi i\).
  13. Question 13Easy
    The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (every non-constant polynomial has a root in \(\mathbb C\)) is most directly proved by applying Liouville’s theorem to

    Why

    If \(p\) had no zero, \(1/p\) would be entire, and \(|p(z)|\to\infty\) makes \(1/p\to0\) at infinity, so it is bounded; Liouville then makes \(1/p\) constant, a contradiction.
