What this unit covers

Five topics, in the order of the official syllabus line:

  1. Analytic functions: limits, complex differentiability, Cauchy-Riemann equations, harmonic functions and conjugates, Milne-Thomson, \(e^z\), \(\log\), powers, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions.
  2. Cauchy theory: contour integrals, Cauchy-Goursat, integral formula, Morera, Liouville, fundamental theorem of algebra, maximum modulus, Schwarz lemma.
  3. Series and singularities: Taylor and Laurent series, zeros, identity theorem, removable/pole/essential singularities, the point at infinity, meromorphic functions.
  4. Residues and contour integration: residue formulas, residue theorem, trigonometric and real-line integrals, Jordan’s lemma, argument principle, Rouché.
  5. Conformal and Möbius maps: conformality, Möbius group, cross-ratio, standard images.

How it is asked

About \(6\) to \(9\) questions per paper (2019: \(9\), 2025: \(6\), 2026: \(7\)). The questions are short and formula-based:

  • evaluate a contour or real integral by residues (2025 Q63, Q65; 2026 Q92) or give a residue (2025 Q61);
  • find \(f\) from \(u\) (2025 Q64) or decide whether \(f\) exists (2026 Q100);
  • name or apply a theorem: Liouville (2019 Q33, 2026 Q86), Morera’s hypotheses, the Green’s-theorem argument for \(\oint\bar f\,dz\) (2026 Q95), conformality (2026 Q70), zeros and poles (2019, 2025 Q66);
  • count zeros with Rouché (2019, 2026 Q77);
  • images under Möbius maps and cross-ratio (2019, 2025 Q62, 2026 Q81);
  • a series fact: radius of convergence (2019 Q34), principal value of \(\log\) (2019 Q31).

Statements with exact hypotheses are the usual trap: “bounded entire”, “continuous with zero integral on every closed curve”, “analytic on a simply connected domain”.

High-yield list

  1. Milne-Thomson from a given real or imaginary part (2025 Q64 type).
  2. Residue at an essential singularity from a Laurent series (\(z\cos\frac1z\): \(-\frac12\)).
  3. Cauchy formula for \(\oint\frac{e^{2z}}{(z+1)^4}\) (2025 Q63 type).
  4. \(\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{a+b\sin\theta}=\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt{a^2-b^2}}\) (2025 Q65 type); watch for integrals that are simply \(0\) (2026 Q92).
  5. Liouville and its variants (\(f/z^3\) bounded entire, 2026 Q86).
  6. Morera’s hypotheses, the Green’s-theorem argument for \(\oint\bar f\,dz\) (2026 Q95), and the conformality condition (Q70).
  7. Rouché zero counts for polynomials (\(z^4+6z+3\), \(z^5-2z+1\)).
  8. Image of the real axis and of circles under Möbius maps (2025 Q62, 2026 Q81).
  9. Cross-ratio is real iff the four points are concyclic or collinear.
  10. Zeros are isolated, no infinite-order zeros; \(e^z/(e^z-1)\) is meromorphic with simple poles at \(2\pi ik\).
  11. Radius of convergence \(\sum(3+4i)^nz^n=\frac15\); principal value \(\operatorname{Log}(-1+i\sqrt3)=\ln2+\frac{2\pi i}3\).
  12. Which standard functions are entire: polynomial and \(\cosh z\) yes, \(\bar z\) and \(\tan z\) no.

Analytic functions, Cauchy - Riemann equations, Cauchy's theorem, Morera's theorem, Liouville's theorem, Cauchy's Integral formula, zeros of analytic functions, Taylor series, Laurent series, Calculus of residues, contour integration, conformal mappings, Mobius transformations.
