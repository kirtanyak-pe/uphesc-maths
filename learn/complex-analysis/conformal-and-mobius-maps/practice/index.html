Conformal mappings and Mobius transformations

  1. Question 1Easy
    The function \(f(z)=z^3-3z\) is analytic everywhere but fails to be conformal exactly at

    Why

    A map analytic at \(z_0\) is conformal there iff \(f'(z_0)\ne0\). \(f'(z)=3z^2-3=0\) at \(z=\pm1\).
  2. Question 2Easy
    The map \(f(z)=e^z\) is conformal

    Why

    \(f'(z)=e^z\neq0\) for every \(z\), so \(f\) is conformal at every point (though not one-to-one, because of the period \(2\pi i\)).
  3. Question 3Medium
    Under \(w=\dfrac1z\) the line \(\operatorname{Re}z=1\) is mapped onto

    Why

    A line not through \(0\) goes to a circle through \(0\). \(z=1\mapsto w=1\) and \(z=\infty\mapsto w=0\) are diametrically opposite points, so the circle is \(|w-\frac12|=\frac12\). Algebraically \(\frac1{1+it}\) has real part \(\frac1{1+t^2}=|w|^2\), so \(|w|^2=\operatorname{Re}w\).
  4. Question 4Medium
    Under \(w=\dfrac{1}{z}\) the circle \(|z-1|=1\) (which passes through \(0\)) is mapped onto

    Why

    A circle through \(0\) becomes a line. The points \(z=2\) and \(z=1\pm i\) go to \(\frac12\) and \(\frac{1\mp i}{2}\), all on \(\operatorname{Re}w=\frac12\).
  5. Question 5Medium
    The Möbius transformation \(w=\dfrac{z-i}{z+i}\) maps the real axis onto

    Why

    For real \(x\), \(|x-i|=|x+i|\), so \(|w|=1\). Also \(z=i\mapsto0\), so the upper half-plane goes onto the open unit disc \(|w|<1\).
  6. Question 6Hard
    The Möbius transformation sending \(0\mapsto-1\), \(1\mapsto-i\), \(\infty\mapsto1\) is

    Why

    \(\infty\mapsto1\) gives the form \(w=\frac{z+\beta}{z+\delta}\). \(0\mapsto-1\) gives \(\beta=-\delta\). Then \(w(1)=\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}=-i\) gives \(\beta=-i\). So \(w=\frac{z-i}{z+i}\). (The other options each fail at least one of the three conditions; e.g. the last sends \(1\mapsto0\).)
  7. Question 7Medium
    The cross-ratio \((z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4)=\dfrac{(z_1-z_3)(z_2-z_4)}{(z_1-z_4)(z_2-z_3)}\) of \(1,\,i,\,-1,\,-i\) is

    Why

    \(\frac{(1-(-1))(i-(-i))}{(1-(-i))(i-(-1))}=\frac{2\cdot2i}{(1+i)^2}=\frac{4i}{2i}=2\). It is real because the four points lie on one circle.
  8. Question 8Medium
    The fixed points of \(w=\dfrac{2z+1}{z+2}\) are

    Why

    \(z=\frac{2z+1}{z+2}\Rightarrow z^2+2z=2z+1\Rightarrow z^2=1\).
  9. Question 9Hard
    Under \(w=\dfrac{5-4z}{4z-2}\) the unit circle \(|z|=1\) is mapped onto the circle with centre and radius

    Why

    \(z=1\mapsto\frac12\) and \(z=-1\mapsto\frac{9}{-6}=-\frac32\). These are symmetric about the real axis (the map has real coefficients), so they are the ends of a diameter on the real line: centre \(-\frac12\), radius \(1\).
  10. Question 10Medium
    If \(S(z)=\dfrac{z+2}{3z+5}\) and \(R(z)=\dfrac{2z-1}{z+1}\), then \(S(R(z))=\)

    Why

    Composition corresponds to matrix product: \(\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\11&2\end{pmatrix}\).
  11. Question 11Medium
    A Möbius transformation of the open unit disc onto itself that maps \(\tfrac12\) to \(0\) is

    Why

    The disc automorphisms are \(e^{i\alpha}\frac{z-a}{1-\bar az}\). With \(a=\frac12\): \(\frac{z-1/2}{1-z/2}=\frac{2z-1}{2-z}\). Check \(|z|=1\Rightarrow|2z-1|=|2-z|\) (since \(|2z-1|^2=5-4\operatorname{Re}z=|2-z|^2\)).
  12. Question 12Medium
    The cross-ratio of four distinct points \(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4\) is real if and only if

    Why

    A Möbius map sends \(z_2,z_3,z_4\mapsto\) points of the real axis and the cross-ratio is the image of \(z_1\); it is real iff \(z_1\) lies on the circle (or line) through the other three. Example: \(0,1,i,1+i\) (corners of a square) give a real value.
  13. Question 13Medium
    The map \(w=e^z\) sends the strip \(0<\operatorname{Im}z<\pi\) onto

    Why

    \(e^{x+iy}=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)\) has modulus \(e^x\in(0,\infty)\) and argument \(y\in(0,\pi)\): every point of the open upper half-plane exactly once.
