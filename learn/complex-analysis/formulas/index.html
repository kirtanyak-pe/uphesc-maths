Unit 4 · Complex Analysis

Formula sheet

Every formula and result of the unit. Each topic page explains where they come from.

Analytic functions.

  • CR: \(u_x=v_y\), \(u_y=-v_x\). Polar: \(u_r=v_\theta/r\), \(v_r=-u_\theta/r\). \(f'=u_x+iv_x=v_y-iu_y\).
  • Milne-Thomson: \(f'(z)=u_x(z,0)-iu_y(z,0)=v_y(z,0)+iv_x(z,0)\).
  • Harmonic: \(u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0\). Conjugate exists on simply connected domains, unique up to a constant.
  • \(e^z=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)\), period \(2\pi i\). \(|\sin z|^2=\sin^2x+\sinh^2y\). \(\sin,\cos\) unbounded.
  • \(\operatorname{Log}z=\ln|z|+i\operatorname{Arg}z\), \(\operatorname{Arg}\in(-\pi,\pi]\), analytic off \((-\infty,0]\). \(z^a=e^{a\log z}\). \(i^i=e^{-\pi/2}\).
  • Entire: polynomials, \(e^z\), \(\sin\), \(\cos\), \(\sinh\), \(\cosh\). Not entire: \(\bar z\), \(\tan z\), \(1/z\), \(\operatorname{Log}z\).

Cauchy theory.

  • ML: \(\left|\int_\gamma f\right|\le ML\). \(\oint_{|z-a|=r}(z-a)^ndz=2\pi i\,\delta_{n,-1}\). \(\oint\bar z\,dz=2i\cdot\text{Area}\).
  • \(f^{(n)}(a)=\frac{n!}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{f(z)}{(z-a)^{n+1}}dz\). Cauchy estimate \(|f^{(n)}(a)|\le n!M/R^n\).
  • Mean value: \(f(a)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}f(a+re^{i\theta})d\theta\).
  • Morera: continuous with \(\oint_T f=0\) for all triangles implies analytic.
  • Liouville: bounded entire implies constant. Max modulus: for non-constant analytic \(f\) on a domain, \(|f|\) has no interior maximum.
  • Schwarz: \(f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D\), \(f(0)=0\): \(|f(z)|\le|z|\), \(|f'(0)|\le1\), equality iff rotation.

Series and singularities.

  • \(1/R=\limsup|a_n|^{1/n}\). Taylor radius = distance to nearest singularity.
  • Laurent: unique per annulus; residue is \(a_{-1}\).
  • Zero of order \(m\): \(f=(z-a)^mg\), \(g(a)\ne0\); zeros of non-zero analytic functions are isolated, never of infinite order.
  • Identity theorem: agreement on a set with a limit point in a domain implies equality.
  • Removable (bounded), pole of order \(m\) (\(|f|\to\infty\)), essential (Casorati-Weierstrass: dense image).
  • At \(\infty\): polynomial = pole; \(e^z\) = essential; meromorphic on \(\widehat{\mathbb C}\) = rational.

Residues.

  • Simple pole: \(\lim(z-a)f=p(a)/q'(a)\). Order \(m\): \(\frac1{(m-1)!}\frac{d^{m-1}}{dz^{m-1}}[(z-a)^mf]_{z=a}\).
  • \(\operatorname{Res}(f,\infty)=-a_{-1}\) at \(\infty\); all residues sum to \(0\).
  • \(\oint_Cf=2\pi i\sum\operatorname{Res}\).
  • \(z=e^{i\theta}\): \(d\theta=\frac{dz}{iz}\), \(\cos\theta=\frac{z+z^{-1}}2\), \(\sin\theta=\frac{z-z^{-1}}{2i}\); \(\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{a+b\cos\theta}=\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt{a^2-b^2}}\).
  • \(\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac PQ=2\pi i\sum_{\text{UHP}}\operatorname{Res}\) (\(\deg Q\ge\deg P+2\)). Jordan: \(\int\frac{\cos ax}{x^2+b^2}=\frac{\pi e^{-ab}}b\).
  • Argument principle: \(\frac1{2\pi i}\oint\frac{f'}f=N-P\). Rouché: \(|g|<|f|\) on \(C\) gives equal zero counts for \(f\) and \(f+g\).

Conformal and Möbius.

  • Analytic and conformal iff \(f'\neq0\).
  • \(T=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\), \(ad-bc\neq0\); circles and lines go to circles and lines; three points determine \(T\).
  • Cross-ratio \(\frac{(z_1-z_3)(z_2-z_4)}{(z_1-z_4)(z_2-z_3)}\) is invariant; real iff concyclic or collinear.
  • \(\frac{z-i}{z+i}\): upper half-plane to the disc, real axis to \(|w|=1\). \(e^{i\theta}\frac{z-a}{1-\bar az}\): disc automorphism.
  • \(1/z\): \(|z|=r\to|w|=1/r\); line not through \(0\leftrightarrow\) circle through \(0\).
