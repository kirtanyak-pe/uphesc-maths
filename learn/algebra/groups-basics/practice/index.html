Groups, subgroups and Lagrange's theorem

  1. Question 1Easy
    The order of the element \(15\) in the group \((\mathbb Z_{36},+)\) is

    Why

    \(o(15)=36/\gcd(36,15)=36/3=12\). The value \(3\) is just the gcd.
  2. Question 2Easy
    Which of the following groups has NO subgroup of order \(6\)?

    Why

    \(A_4\) (order 12) has no subgroup of order 6: the converse of Lagrange fails. \(S_3\) is itself of order 6, \(\mathbb Z_{12}\) is cyclic so has one subgroup of each divisor order, \(D_6\) contains its 6 rotations.
  3. Question 3Easy
    The number of elements of order \(2\) in the dihedral group of order \(16\) is

    Why

    The 8 reflections have order 2. Among the rotations \(r^k\) (with \(r\) of order 8) only \(r^4\) has order 2. Total \(8+1=9\). Choosing \(8\) forgets the central rotation \(r^4\).
  4. Question 4Medium
    The number of elements of order \(4\) in \(\mathbb Z_4\times\mathbb Z_4\) is

    Why

    Elements with \(4x=0\): all 16. Those with \(2x=0\): \(2\cdot2=4\) (each coordinate in \(\{0,2\}\)). So \(16-4=12\) elements have order exactly 4.
  5. Question 5Medium
    The order of \(GL_2(\mathbb F_5)\) is

    Why

    Count ordered pairs of independent rows: first row any of \(5^2-1=24\) nonzero vectors, second row outside the line spanned by the first: \(5^2-5=20\). Total \(24\cdot20=480\). (\(600=24\cdot25\) forgets that the second row must avoid the span.)
  6. Question 6Medium
    Consider: (i) a group of order \(6\) has a subgroup of order \(3\); (ii) \(A_4\) has a subgroup of order \(6\); (iii) every group of order \(4\) is cyclic; (iv) every subgroup of index \(2\) is normal. Which statements are true?

    Why

    (i) true: a subgroup of order 3 exists (Cauchy, or the rotations of \(D_3\), or \(\langle 2\rangle\le\mathbb Z_6\)). (ii) false: converse of Lagrange fails in \(A_4\). (iii) false: \(\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\) has order 4 and is not cyclic. (iv) true: left and right cosets coincide.
  7. Question 7Easy
    If \(a\) is an element of order \(18\) in a group, the order of \(a^{15}\) is

    Why

    \(o(a^{15})=18/\gcd(18,15)=18/3=6\).
  8. Question 8Medium
    Assertion (A): every subgroup of index \(2\) in a group is normal. Reason (R): a group of order \(12\) always has a subgroup of order \(6\). Choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true, R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

    Why

    (A) is true: with index 2 there are only two left cosets, \(H\) and its complement, and the same for right cosets, so \(gH=Hg\). (R) is false: \(A_4\) has order 12 and no subgroup of order 6.
  9. Question 9Medium
    A group \(G\) of order \(36\) has subgroups \(H\) and \(K\) of orders \(12\) and \(18\). Then \(\lvert H\cap K\rvert\) equals

    Why

    \(\lvert H\cap K\rvert\) divides \(\gcd(12,18)=6\). Also \(\lvert HK\rvert=12\cdot18/\lvert H\cap K\rvert=216/d\le36\) forces \(d\ge6\). So \(d=6\).
  10. Question 10Medium
    The number of subgroups of order \(2\) in \(\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\) is

    Why

    A subgroup of order 2 is \(\{0,x\}\) with \(x\) of order 2, and every nonzero element has order 2. There are \(2^3-1=7\) such elements, hence 7 subgroups.
  11. Question 11Hard
    Which of the following groups is cyclic?

    Why

    \(U(18)\) has order 6 and \(18=2\cdot3^2\) is of the form \(2p^k\), so it is cyclic (3 is not a generator but 5 is: \(5\) has order 6 mod 18). \(U(8)\) and \(U(12)\) are \(\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\) (all elements square to 1) and \(U(20)\cong\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_4\) has no element of order 8.
  12. Question 12Hard
    Which of the following groups of order \(8\) has exactly one element of order \(2\)?

    Why

    Number of elements of order 2: \(D_4\) has 5, \(\mathbb Z_4\times\mathbb Z_2\) has 3, \(\mathbb Z_2^3\) has 7, \(Q_8\) has 1 (namely \(-1\)). (Cyclic \(\mathbb Z_8\) would also have one, but it is not listed.)
