Homomorphisms, quotient groups and abelian groups

  1. Question 1Easy
    The number of group homomorphisms from \(\mathbb Z_{12}\) to \(\mathbb Z_{18}\) is

    Why

    A homomorphism is fixed by \(x=f(1)\) with \(12x\equiv0\pmod{18}\), i.e. \(x\) a multiple of \(18/\gcd(12,18)=3\). So \(x\in\{0,3,6,9,12,15\}\): \(\gcd(12,18)=6\) homomorphisms.
  2. Question 2Easy
    The quotient group \(\mathbb Z_{12}/\langle4\rangle\) is isomorphic to

    Why

    \(\langle4\rangle=\{0,4,8\}\) has order 3, so the quotient has order 4. It is cyclic, generated by \(1+\langle4\rangle\), which has order 4 (the smallest \(k\) with \(k\in\langle4\rangle\) is 4). Hence \(\mathbb Z_4\). In general \(\mathbb Z_n/\langle d\rangle\cong\mathbb Z_d\).
  3. Question 3Easy
    The number of non-isomorphic abelian groups of order \(36\) is

    Why

    \(36=2^2\cdot3^2\). For each prime square there are \(P(2)=2\) choices (\(\mathbb Z_{p^2}\) or \(\mathbb Z_p\times\mathbb Z_p\)). Total \(2\cdot2=4\).
  4. Question 4Easy
    The quotient group \(GL_n(\mathbb R)/SL_n(\mathbb R)\) is isomorphic to

    Why

    The determinant \(GL_n(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R^\times\) is a surjective homomorphism with kernel \(SL_n(\mathbb R)\). Apply the first isomorphism theorem.
  5. Question 5Medium
    The number of non-isomorphic abelian groups of order \(360\) is

    Why

    \(360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\). Partitions of the exponents: \(P(3)=3\), \(P(2)=2\), \(P(1)=1\). Total \(3\cdot2\cdot1=6\).
  6. Question 6Medium
    The order of the group \(\operatorname{Inn}(D_4)\) of inner automorphisms of the dihedral group of order \(8\) is

    Why

    \(\operatorname{Inn}(G)\cong G/Z(G)\). \(Z(D_4)=\{e,r^2\}\) has order 2, so \(\lvert\operatorname{Inn}(D_4)\rvert=8/2=4\).
  7. Question 7Medium
    Consider: (i) every subgroup of index \(2\) is normal; (ii) a normal subgroup of a normal subgroup of \(G\) is normal in \(G\); (iii) if \(G/Z(G)\) is cyclic then \(G\) is abelian; (iv) every subgroup of an abelian group is normal. Which statements are true?

    Why

    (i) true. (ii) false: \(\{e,(1\,2)(3\,4)\}\trianglelefteq V_4\trianglelefteq A_4\) but it is not normal in \(A_4\). (iii) true (generators of \(G/Z\) lift to give commuting representatives). (iv) true since \(gHg^{-1}=H\) when \(G\) is abelian.
  8. Question 8Medium
    The order of the element \(7+\langle6\rangle\) in the quotient group \(\mathbb Z_{24}/\langle6\rangle\) is

    Why

    \(\langle6\rangle=\{0,6,12,18\}\), so the quotient has order 6 and is \(\cong\mathbb Z_6\). The order of \(7+\langle6\rangle\) is the least \(k\) with \(7k\in\langle6\rangle\), i.e. \(6\mid7k\), so \(k=6\). (Generally \(o(k+\langle d\rangle)=d/\gcd(d,k)=6/1\).)
  9. Question 9Medium
    Assertion (A): if every subgroup of a group \(G\) is normal, then \(G\) is abelian. Reason (R): every subgroup of an abelian group is normal. Choose the correct option.

    Why

    (R) is true: in an abelian group \(gHg^{-1}=H\). (A) is false: \(Q_8\) is nonabelian but every subgroup of it is normal. R is the converse of what A claims, so it does not support A.
  10. Question 10Medium
    The order of the automorphism group of \(\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_3\) is

    Why

    \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb Z_p\times\mathbb Z_p)\cong GL_2(\mathbb F_p)\), of order \((p^2-1)(p^2-p)\). For \(p=3\) this is \(8\cdot6=48\). A common slip is to use \(\lvert\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb Z_3)\rvert^2=4\), which only counts automorphisms that act separately on each factor.
  11. Question 11Hard
    The number of group homomorphisms from \(S_3\) to \(S_3\) is

    Why

    Classify by kernel. Kernel \(S_3\): 1 trivial homomorphism. Kernel \(A_3\): \(S_3/A_3\cong\mathbb Z_2\) embeds in \(S_3\) in 3 ways (choose the image of order 2 among 3 transpositions): 3 homomorphisms. Kernel trivial: automorphisms, \(\lvert\operatorname{Aut}(S_3)\rvert=6\). There are no other normal subgroups. Total \(1+3+6=10\).
  12. Question 12Hard
    The number of injective group homomorphisms from \(\mathbb Z_6\) to \(\mathbb Z_{30}\) is

    Why

    \(f(1)=x\) must have order exactly 6 in \(\mathbb Z_{30}\) (then \(f\) is injective). \(\mathbb Z_{30}\) has \(\varphi(6)=2\) elements of order 6, namely \(5\) and \(25\). So 2 injective homomorphisms. (There are \(\gcd(6,30)=6\) homomorphisms in all.)
  13. Question 13Hard
    The automorphism group of the cyclic group \(\mathbb Z_{15}\) is isomorphic to

    Why

    \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb Z_{15})\cong U(15)\cong U(3)\times U(5)\cong\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_4\), of order \(\varphi(15)=8\). It is not cyclic (no element of order 8: the orders are \(1,2,2,2,4,4,4,4\)) and not elementary abelian (it has elements of order 4).
