Calculus of variations

  • \(J=\int_a^bF(x,y,y')dx\); E-L: \(F_y-\dfrac d{dx}F_{y'}=0\) (second order, non-linear in general).
  • \(F\) free of \(y\): \(F_{y'}=C\). \(F\) free of \(x\): \(F-y'F_{y'}=C\). \(F\) free of \(y'\): \(F_y=0\).
  • Several functions: \(F_{y_i}-\frac d{dx}F_{y_i'}=0\). Euler-Poisson: \(\sum(-1)^k\frac{d^k}{dx^k}F_{y^{(k)}}=0\). Two variables: \(F_u-\partial_xF_{u_x}-\partial_yF_{u_y}=0\).
  • Isoperimetric: E-L for \(F+\lambda G\) with \(\int G=\ell\).
  • Free end: \(F_{y'}=0\). End on \(y=\varphi(x)\): \(F+(\varphi'-y')F_{y'}=0\).
  • Legendre: min needs \(F_{y'y'}\ge0\); Jacobi: no conjugate point; convex \(F\) gives global min.
  • Brachistochrone: cycloid. Minimal surface of revolution: catenary \(y=c\cosh\frac{x-x_0}c\). Plane geodesic: line.

Integral equations

  • Fredholm: \(\varphi=f+\lambda\int_a^bK\varphi\,dt\). Volterra: upper limit \(x\). First kind: no \(\varphi(x)\) outside.
  • Separable \(K=\sum a_i(x)b_i(t)\): \(c_i=\int b_i\varphi\), \((I-\lambda A)\mathbf c=\mathbf f\), \(A_{ki}=\int b_ka_i\), eigenvalues from \(\det(I-\lambda A)=0\).
  • Fredholm alternative: \(D(\lambda)\ne0\) gives a unique solution for every \(f\); \(D(\lambda)=0\) requires \(f\perp\) adjoint eigenfunctions (rank condition).
  • Iterated kernels \(K_{n+1}=\int K(x,s)K_n(s,t)ds\); resolvent \(R=\sum\lambda^nK_{n+1}\); \(\varphi=f+\lambda\int Rf\).
  • Neumann series converges if \(\lvert\lambda\rvert M(b-a)<1\); Volterra converges for all \(\lambda\) and has no eigenvalues.
  • Symmetric kernel: real eigenvalues, orthogonal eigenfunctions; rank-\(n\) symmetric kernel has \(n\) eigenvalues; \(\varphi=f+\lambda\sum\frac{(f,\varphi_n)}{\lambda_n-\lambda}\varphi_n\).
  • Fredholm determinant: non-trivial solution of \(\varphi=\lambda T\varphi\) iff \(D(\lambda)=0\).
  • IVP \(y''+By=g\): \(y=c_0+c_1x+\int_0^x(x-t)g-\int_0^x(x-t)By\). Leibniz: \(\frac d{dx}\int_a^xF=F(x,x)+\int_a^xF_x\).
  • Green’s function: continuous, \(L[G]=0\) off the diagonal, boundary conditions, jump \(1/p(t)\) in \(G_x\); \(G=c\,u_1(x_<)u_2(x_>)\), \(c=1/(pW)\).
  • Dirichlet \(y''+\lambda y=0\) on \([0,1]\): kernel \(x_<(1-x_>)\), \(\lambda_n=n^2\pi^2\). Kernel \(xt\) on \([0,1]\): \(\lambda=3\).
  • Convolution Volterra: second kind \(\Phi=F/(1-\lambda\kappa)\); first kind \(\Phi=F/\kappa\).

Laplace transforms

  • \(\mathscr L\{t^n\}=n!/s^{n+1}\), \(e^{at}\to\frac1{s-a}\), \(\sin at\to\frac a{s^2+a^2}\), \(\cos at\to\frac s{s^2+a^2}\), \(\sinh at\to\frac a{s^2-a^2}\), \(\cosh at\to\frac s{s^2-a^2}\).
  • \(e^{at}F\to f(s-a)\); \(F(t-a)H(t-a)\to e^{-as}f\); \(F(at)\to\frac1af(s/a)\).
  • \(F'\to sf-F(0)\); \(F''\to s^2f-sF(0)-F'(0)\); \(\int_0^tF\to f/s\).
  • \(t^nF\to(-1)^nf^{(n)}\); \(F/t\to\int_s^\infty f\); periodic: \(\int_0^Te^{-st}F\,dt/(1-e^{-sT})\).
  • \(\delta(t-a)\to e^{-as}\); \(H(t-a)\to e^{-as}/s\); \(F*G\to fg\).
  • \(F(0^+)=\lim_{s\to\infty}sf\); \(F(\infty)=\lim_{s\to0}sf\) if the limit exists.
  • \(\mathscr L\{t\cos at\}=\frac{s^2-a^2}{(s^2+a^2)^2}\); \(\mathscr L\{t\sin at\}=\frac{2as}{(s^2+a^2)^2}\); \(\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin t}t\,dt=\frac\pi2\).
