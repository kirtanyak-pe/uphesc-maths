What this unit covers

Finite-dimensional linear algebra over \(\mathbb{R}\) and \(\mathbb{C}\), in seven topics.

  1. Vector spaces (the base for everything): subspaces, span, basis, dimension, sums, direct sums, quotients.
  2. Linear transformations: kernel, range, rank-nullity, matrix of a map, change of basis, similarity, idempotent and nilpotent maps, rank of products.
  3. Dual spaces and linear systems: dual basis, annihilators, transpose map, rank, consistency of \(Ax=b\), dimension of the solution space.
  4. Eigenvalues and diagonalisation: characteristic and minimal polynomials, Cayley-Hamilton, multiplicities, diagonalisability, special matrices.
  5. Canonical forms: Jordan and rational canonical forms, invariant factors.
  6. Inner product spaces: orthogonality, Gram-Schmidt, adjoint, Hermitian, skew-Hermitian and unitary matrices, spectral theorem.
  7. Quadratic forms: matrix of a form, reduction by congruence, rank, index, signature, definiteness.

This is the most predictable unit of the paper: 8 questions in 2025 (Q46-Q53), 10 in 2026, and 7 recovered from 2019. Questions are short computations or true/false statements, and many can be done from eigenvalues alone.

How it is asked

  • Statement-picking. “Which of the following is not necessarily true?” with three or four statements about dimension, invertibility, eigenvalues or idempotents (2019 had four of these). Each option differs from a true theorem by one hypothesis, so know the exact conditions.
  • One-line computations. Eigenvalues of a \(3\times3\) matrix (2025 Q48), rank or nullity from given data (2025 Q46, 2025 Q50, 2025 Q51), dimension of a solution space (2026 Q90).
  • Structure from an identity. \(A^2=A\), \(A^2=I\), \(A^3-3A^2+2A=0\): read off eigenvalues, then trace, rank, nullity or determinant (2026 Q80, Q83, Q98).
  • Quadratic forms. Write the matrix, find rank and index, or the signature of a symmetric matrix from its characteristic polynomial (2026 Q84, Q87).
  • Canonical forms. Count Jordan blocks or Jordan forms for given data (2026 Q31, Q93).
  • Inner products. Orthogonal complements, projections, Gram-Schmidt, properties of unitary or Hermitian matrices (2025 Q52, 2026 Q38, 2019).

High-yield list

  • Eigenvalue questions from trace, determinant or a polynomial identity (\(A^2=A\), \(A^2=I\), \(A^3-3A^2+2A=0\)): asked every year.
  • Rank-nullity and the dimension of \(\ker\) and range for a given operator.
  • Idempotent operators: \(V=\ker\oplus\operatorname{range}\), trace equals rank (asked 2019, 2026).
  • Diagonalisability: distinct eigenvalues versus geometric multiplicity; a repeated eigenvalue does not forbid it.
  • Dimension of a solution space, rank depending on a parameter, consistency of \(Ax=b\).
  • Dual basis and annihilator dimensions.
  • Signature, rank and index of a quadratic form; definiteness by eigenvalues or leading minors.
  • Jordan form counts: number of blocks equals \(g(\lambda)\), largest block equals the exponent in \(m_A\).
  • Hermitian, skew-Hermitian and unitary eigenvalue facts, and the orthogonal complement identities.
  • Statements with “not necessarily true”: check each against the identity matrix and a non-zero nilpotent matrix.
