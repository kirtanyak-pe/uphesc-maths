Unit 2 · Linear Algebra

Formula sheet

Every formula and result of the unit. Each topic page explains where they come from.

Vector spaces

  • \(\dim(U+W)=\dim U+\dim W-\dim(U\cap W)\); \(V=U\oplus W\) iff \(U\cap W=0\) and \(\dim U+\dim W=\dim V\).
  • \(\dim V/W=\dim V-\dim W\); \(\dim(U\cap W)\ge\dim U+\dim W-\dim V\).
  • \(\dim M_n=n^2\); symmetric \(\tfrac{n(n+1)}2\); skew-symmetric \(\tfrac{n(n-1)}2\); trace zero \(n^2-1\); \(\dim P_n=n+1\); \(\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C^n=2n\).
  • Over \(F_q\): \(\lvert F_q^n\rvert=q^n\); number of ordered bases \(\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(q^n-q^i)\); number of lines \((q^n-1)/(q-1)\).
  • \(U\cup W\) is a subspace iff one contains the other.

Linear transformations

  • \(\dim V=\operatorname{nullity}T+\operatorname{rank}T\) (nullity of the domain).
  • \(\dim L(V,W)=\dim V\cdot\dim W\).
  • Column \(j\) of \([T]_{B,C}\) is \([T(b_j)]_C\); \([T]_{B'}=P^{-1}[T]_BP\).
  • Similar matrices share trace, determinant, rank, \(\chi\) and \(m\).
  • \(\operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{rank}B-n\le\operatorname{rank}AB\le\min(\operatorname{rank}A,\operatorname{rank}B)\).
  • \(\operatorname{rank}(A+B)\le\operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{rank}B\).
  • \(P^2=P\): \(V=\ker P\oplus\operatorname{range}P\), eigenvalues \(0,1\), \(\operatorname{tr}P=\operatorname{rank}P\).
  • Nilpotent \(N\) of index \(k\): \(N^n=0\), \(\operatorname{tr}N=0\), \((I-N)^{-1}=I+N+\cdots+N^{k-1}\); \(N^2=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{rank}N\le n/2\).

Dual spaces and linear systems

  • \(\dim V^*=\dim V\); dual basis \(f_i(v_j)=\delta_{ij}\), given by the rows of \(P^{-1}\) when the basis vectors are the columns of \(P\).
  • \(\dim W^0=\dim V-\dim W\); \((W_1+W_2)^0=W_1^0\cap W_2^0\); \((W_1\cap W_2)^0=W_1^0+W_2^0\).
  • \(\ker T^t=(\operatorname{range}T)^0\); \(\operatorname{range}T^t=(\ker T)^0\); \(\operatorname{rank}T^t=\operatorname{rank}T\).
  • \(\ker f\subseteq\ker g\Rightarrow g=cf\).
  • \(Ax=b\) is consistent iff \(\operatorname{rank}A=\operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]\); the solution set is empty or an affine space of dimension \(n-\operatorname{rank}A\); unique iff the rank is \(n\).
  • \(\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{adj}A)=n,1,0\) according as \(\operatorname{rank}A=n,\ n-1,\ <n-1\).
  • \(Ax=0\) has a non-trivial solution iff \(\operatorname{rank}A<n\) (for square \(A\): iff \(\det A=0\)).

Eigenvalues and diagonalisation

  • \(\chi_A(x)=\det(xI-A)=x^n-(\operatorname{tr}A)x^{n-1}+\cdots+(-1)^n\det A\).
  • \(\sum\lambda_i=\operatorname{tr}A\), \(\prod\lambda_i=\det A\), \(\operatorname{tr}A^k=\sum\lambda_i^k\).
  • \(1\le g(\lambda)\le a(\lambda)\), with \(g(\lambda)=n-\operatorname{rank}(A-\lambda I)\).
  • Cayley-Hamilton: \(\chi_A(A)=0\); \(m_A\mid\chi_A\); same roots.
  • \(2\times2\): \(A^2=(\operatorname{tr}A)A-(\det A)I\) and \(A^{-1}=\dfrac{(\operatorname{tr}A)I-A}{\det A}\).
  • Diagonalisable iff \(m_A\) has distinct linear factors iff \(\sum g(\lambda)=n\). Distinct eigenvalues is sufficient, not necessary.
  • Eigenvalues: \(p(A)\to p(\lambda)\); \(A^{-1}\to1/\lambda\); \(\operatorname{adj}A\to\det A/\lambda\); \(A^T\) same as \(A\); \(AB\) and \(BA\) have the same \(\chi\).
  • \(A^2=A\): eigenvalues \(0,1\); \(A^2=I\): eigenvalues \(\pm1\); nilpotent: only \(0\); rank one \(uv^T\): \(v^Tu\) and \(0\)’s.
  • \(\det(\operatorname{adj}A)=(\det A)^{n-1}\); \(\det(I+uv^T)=1+v^Tu\).
  • Tridiagonal \(2,-1,-1\): \(\det=n+1\).

Canonical forms

  • Jordan blocks for \(\lambda\): their number is \(g(\lambda)\), their sizes sum to \(a(\lambda)\), the largest equals the exponent of \((x-\lambda)\) in \(m_A\).
  • Number of blocks of size exactly \(k\): \(r_{k-1}-2r_k+r_{k+1}\) with \(r_k=\operatorname{rank}(A-\lambda I)^k\).
  • Number of Jordan forms for a given \(\chi=\prod(x-\lambda_i)^{a_i}\): \(\prod p(a_i)\) (partition numbers); \(\chi\) and \(m\) determine the form when \(n\le3\), not for \(n=4\) in general.
  • Nilpotent \(n\times n\) classes: \(p(n)\); \(N\sim J_n(0)\) iff \(N^{n-1}\ne0\).
  • Invariant factors \(d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_k\): \(\chi=\prod d_i\), \(m=d_k\), \(\sum\deg d_i=n\).
  • Companion matrix \(C(f)\) has \(\chi=m=f\). Rational form exists over any field; Jordan form needs \(\chi\) to split.
  • \(J_2(\lambda)^m=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda^m&m\lambda^{m-1}\\0&\lambda^m\end{pmatrix}\).

Inner product spaces

  • Cauchy-Schwarz: \(\lvert\langle x,y\rangle\rvert\le\lVert x\rVert\lVert y\rVert\), equality iff \(x,y\) dependent.
  • Parallelogram law: \(\lVert x+y\rVert^2+\lVert x-y\rVert^2=2\lVert x\rVert^2+2\lVert y\rVert^2\).
  • Gram-Schmidt: \(u_j=v_j-\sum_{i<j}\dfrac{\langle v_j,u_i\rangle}{\langle u_i,u_i\rangle}u_i\).
  • Orthonormal expansion \(x=\sum\langle x,e_i\rangle e_i\); Parseval \(\lVert x\rVert^2=\sum\lvert\langle x,e_i\rangle\rvert^2\).
  • \(\dim W+\dim W^\perp=\dim V\); \(W^{\perp\perp}=W\); \(S\subseteq S^{\perp\perp}\).
  • Projection onto a line: \(vv^T/v^Tv\); onto the column space of \(A\): \(A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T\). Orthogonal projections satisfy \(P^2=P=P^*\).
  • Adjoint: \(\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,T^*y\rangle\); \((ST)^*=T^*S^*\); \(\ker T^*=(\operatorname{range}T)^\perp\).
  • Hermitian: real eigenvalues. Skew-Hermitian: purely imaginary or \(0\). Unitary: \(\lvert\lambda\rvert=1\) and \(\lvert\det\rvert=1\). Real orthogonal: \(\det=\pm1\).
  • Normal (\(AA^*=A^*A\)) iff unitarily diagonalisable; real symmetric: orthogonally diagonalisable with real eigenvalues; eigenvectors of distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal.
  • Cayley transform: \(K\) skew-Hermitian implies \((I-K)(I+K)^{-1}\) unitary.

Quadratic forms

  • Matrix of \(Q\): \(a_{ii}\) is the coefficient of \(x_i^2\), \(a_{ij}\) is half the coefficient of \(x_ix_j\).
  • Congruence \(B=P^TAP\) (\(P\) invertible) preserves rank, definiteness and the pair \((p,q)\).
  • Rank \(r=p+q\), index \(p\) (number of positive squares), signature \(p-q\). Sylvester’s law of inertia: \((p,q)\) does not depend on the reduction used.
  • Number of real congruence classes of forms in \(n\) variables: \(\tfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}2\); complex: \(n+1\).
  • For symmetric \(A\), \(p\) and \(q\) are the numbers of positive and negative eigenvalues.
  • Positive definite iff all eigenvalues \(>0\) iff all leading minors \(\Delta_k>0\) iff \(A=B^TB\) with \(B\) invertible. Negative definite iff \((-1)^k\Delta_k>0\). Positive semidefinite iff all principal minors \(\ge0\) (not only the leading ones).
  • Rayleigh: \(\lambda_{\min}\le x^TAx\le\lambda_{\max}\) for \(\lVert x\rVert=1\).
  • Conics: \(ax^2+2hxy+by^2+\cdots\) with \(\delta=ab-h^2\); rotation angle \(\tan2\theta=\dfrac{2h}{a-b}\).
