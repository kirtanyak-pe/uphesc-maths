  • Gaussian curvature of a surface

    Show answerHide answer

    A sphere of radius r has K = 1/r². For z = f(x, y), K = (f_xx f_yy − f_xy²) / (1 + f_x² + f_y²)², so z = x² + y² has K = 4 at the origin.

Exam-day reminders

  • No negative marking. Never leave a question blank.
  • 100 questions in 120 minutes. Spend about 20 minutes on the general section, which leaves about 85 seconds per maths question.
  • For “which is not true”, check every option before you choose.
  • Two-step questions: write the first result down before you move on.
  • A faulty question is usually given as bonus marks. If no option fits, pick the closest and move on.
