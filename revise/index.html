  • Which Poisson-bracket identity fails

    Show answerHide answer

    [qᵢ, pⱼ] = δᵢⱼ and [qᵢ, qⱼ] = [pᵢ, pⱼ] = 0. Brackets are antisymmetric and obey Jacobi.
