Question 31 of 120Abstract Algebra

The number of elements in the splitting field of \(x^3-2\) over \(\mathbb Z_5\) is

Question 33 of 120Abstract Algebra

Question 34 of 120Abstract Algebra

Question 36 of 120Abstract Algebra

Question 37 of 120Abstract Algebra

The smallest \(n\) such that the quaternion group \(Q_8\) is isomorphic to a subgroup of \(S_n\) is

\(Q_8\) has a unique involution \(-1\). In a faithful action on \(\{1,\dots,n\}\), some orbit must be moved nontrivially by \(-1\); the stabiliser of a point in that orbit cannot contain \(-1\), hence is trivial (every nontrivial subgroup of \(Q_8\) contains \(-1\)), so that orbit has 8 points and \(n\ge8\). Cayley gives \(n=8\). (Computer check: \(S_7\) contains no copy of \(Q_8\).) The tempting answer 4 belongs to \(D_4\), not \(Q_8\).

Question 38 of 120Abstract Algebra

R is true: \(N(2)=4\), and no element has norm \(2\), so \(2\) cannot split into two nonunits. A is false: \(2\mid6=(1+\sqrt{-5})(1-\sqrt{-5})\) but \(2\) divides neither factor, since \((1\pm\sqrt{-5})/2\notin\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}]\). Irreducible does not imply prime outside a UFD.

Question 39 of 120Abstract Algebra

Question 53 of 120Real and Functional Analysis

Which pair of norms is NOT equivalent?

Question 64 of 120Complex Analysis

Question 65 of 120Complex Analysis
