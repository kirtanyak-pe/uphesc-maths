Five fixed papers laid out like the real exam: general studies first, then maths unit by unit. Sit one in a single go.

How it works

  • The 19 and 20 November 2026 paper has 120 questions in 120 minutes: 30 on general studies, then 90 on maths.
  • Each correct answer is worth 3 marks, so the best score is 360. Each wrong answer loses 1.
  • A blind guess gains nothing on average. Guess once you can rule out one option.
  • The timer starts when you press Start and keeps running if you leave. Open the same test again to carry on.
  • Your answers and scores stay in this browser only.
