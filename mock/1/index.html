Question 71 of 120Topology and Differential Geometry

Using the standard definitions (\(T_3\) means regular and \(T_1\)), which implication holds for every topological space?

The cofinite topology on an infinite set shows \(T_1\not\Rightarrow T_2\), the Sierpinski space shows \(T_0\not\Rightarrow T_1\), and the \(K\)-topology on \(\mathbb R\) shows \(T_2\not\Rightarrow T_3\).
